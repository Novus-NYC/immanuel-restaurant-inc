## 0. Read this first (things that change the build)

1. **Their current websites are both down.** immanuelrest.com is a WordPress site that returns a "critical error" page (PHP version mismatch) on every page as of Nov 3, 2026 (via direct visit). immanuelrestaurantinc.com redirects to a Slice "disabled shop" notice, and Slice says the restaurant is no longer active there (via direct visit, Slice menu page). Google lists **no website** for the business ("Add website" prompt) (via Google Maps).
2. **They already have a first-party online ordering page.** order.online/store/397994 is a DoorDash Storefront ("Powered by DoorDash Commerce Platform") for pickup and delivery (via order.online search result). This is the natural "Order Online" button target. Marketplace links are listed in Section 2.
3. **Three different phone numbers are in circulation.** Use 347-240-4703 (the one you were given, and the one Google, Grubhub, Restaurantji and the NYC Health Dept. record all use). Details in Section 2.
4. **Hours conflict across platforms.** The most consistent set is Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat 7 AM to 7 PM, Wed 7 AM to 5 PM, Sun closed. Their own 2024 Instagram graphic says weekdays open at 6 AM. Confirm with the owner before launch. Details in Section 2.
5. **Owner's first name is spelled three ways online.** Yonayka (her restaurant's own Instagram), Yonaika (Panamanian Parade bio), Jonayka (Outgoing listing). Confirm spelling.
6. **DoorDash item descriptions are AI-generated by DoorDash**, not written by the restaurant (DoorDash labels each one "Description generated by DoorDash using AI"). Several are factually wrong. Do not copy them onto the site without checking. Examples in Section 3.
7. **Recent good-news hooks for the homepage.** Brooklyn Chamber of Commerce small business grant, Oct 2026. Panamanian Parade honoree, 2025. Details in Section 5.

## 3. Products / Services / Menu

### 3.1 How to read the menu data

- **Master list** DoorDash, captured in full from the page data (26 sections, 212 listings, 200 unique items; some items appear in more than one section, for example in "Most Ordered").
- **Grubhub prices** were matched item by item. "Not captured on Grubhub" means the item did not show up in my Grubhub capture. Grubhub loads its menu in chunks while scrolling, so a few items may exist there but were missed. Grubhub prices are often different, and Grubhub "Plates" are about $3 higher with a "+" sign (meaning options can add more). Both platforms say the merchant sets the prices and that prices may differ from in-store.
- **In-store prices** NOT FOUND. No current in-store or printed menu was found online. Ask the owner which price list the website should show.
- **Options / modifiers** I opened 32 representative items on DoorDash (at least one per section) and recorded every option group. Items marked "Not opened" were not individually inspected. Patterns that repeat across a section are summarized in 3.3.
- **Descriptions** Item-level descriptions on DoorDash are written by DoorDash's AI, not the restaurant. Section notes (for example "Served with Rice & Beans") are the restaurant's own text. Several AI descriptions are wrong, for example
  - "2 Eggs with Fish" is described as turkey bacon strips
  - "2 Eggs with Shrimp" is described as turkey sausage
  - "Tuna Sandwich" is described as turkey bacon and eggs on a bagel
  - "Double Cheeseburger" says it comes with fries, yet fries are a +$3.00 add-on
  - Soul Food Specials descriptions mention grits, coleslaw or cabbage, while the section note says mac and cheese, collard greens and candy yams
- DoorDash "store bio" calls the restaurant a pizzeria with "the best pizza in Brooklyn" (via DoorDash). That is auto-generated and wrong. Do not reuse.
